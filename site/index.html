Exams, deadlines and grades on one calendar

Study Desk is a planner for students. Keep your calendar, to-dos, grades and shortcuts together, learn new topics with an AI tutor, and keep your phone and PC in step.

Download See what's inside

Free and open source. For Windows, Mac and Android.

See the whole term at a glance

Put exams, assignment deadlines and project schedules on one month view. Open a day to see what is due and edit it in place.

  • Exam
  • Assignment
  • Project

Each type has its own color, so a heavy week shows up before it arrives.

Know what is still open

Add an assignment with its course and due date, then tick it off when it is done. Overdue work is marked, and every due date also appears on the calendar.

  • Filter by all, pending or done.
  • See how many days are left at a glance.

Keep track of how you are doing

Record each exam score by course. Study Desk works out your average per course and overall, shows your best result, and reminds you which past exams still need a score.

Explain it out loud

In any AI Tutor lesson, press Voice tutor. The tutor reads a short script, then asks you to explain the topic in your own words and answers what you wrote.

  • Works right away with your computer's own voice.
  • In the Windows and Mac programs you can download a natural voice once. It runs on your computer, in English or in about 30 other languages, including Turkish.

In the Windows and Mac programs you can also speak your answer. Choose Push to talk (press, talk, press again) or Mic open (the microphone opens on your turn and stops when you do). English now; Turkish after one more check. Not on Android yet.

Keep your phone and PC in step

Open Sync on the PC, choose how the two should find each other, and scan the code with your phone. After that, a change on one device shows up on the other.

It brings across your calendar entries, to-dos, shortcuts and saved AI courses. Your AI key and settings never leave the device they are on.

Everything is encrypted with a key that only your two devices hold. The key is in the code you scan.

Same Wi-Fi
No account and nothing to set up. Both apps are open on the same network.
Over the internet
Works on mobile data and across networks. You set up a small free relay on your own Cloudflare account once. It stores only an encrypted package.
Both
Uses the Wi-Fi at home and the internet when you are away.

          Questions

          Where is my planner data kept?

          On your own device. Study Desk does not upload your planner to us. If you turn on Sync, your devices pass the data to each other in encrypted form.

          Does Study Desk cost anything?

          No. The programs are free and the source code is public under the MIT license. If you use the AI Tutor, the AI service you choose may charge for its own use or limit it.

          Does the Voice tutor send my voice anywhere?

          Only if you turn it on. In the Windows and Mac programs you can speak your answer after downloading a listener once. Your voice is turned into text on your own computer and is not sent anywhere; the recording is not kept after it is understood. The microphone is only used while the Voice tutor card is listening, and the program asks your system for permission first. The tutor's script is written by the AI service you chose, like the rest of the AI Tutor. The downloaded voices turn that script into sound on your own computer. The computer's own voice is whatever your system provides, and some systems' voices use the internet.

          What does the AI service get to see?

          Only what the AI Tutor needs for the course you ask for: your topic, the lesson text, and any question or answer you type. It goes to the service you picked, using your own key. Your calendar, to-dos and grades are not part of those requests. With a local model in the Windows or Mac program, it stays on your computer.

          Is there an iPhone version?

          Not yet. Study Desk is available for Windows, Mac and Android.

          My system warns me about the program. Is it safe to open?

          The programs are not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows and macOS warn about them. The steps to open them anyway are under each system in the download section above. You can read the source code and check the build on GitHub before you install.

          How do I set up Sync over the internet?

          You deploy a small relay to a free Cloudflare account once, which takes about ten minutes. Syncing on the same Wi-Fi needs no setup.
